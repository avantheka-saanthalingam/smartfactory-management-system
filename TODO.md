# SmartFactory delivery outcomes

- [ ] **Role-based access and dashboard:** Secure JWT authentication and input validation; implement Salesman, Admin, Production Supervisor, Owner and read-only Accountant/Auditor permissions. Salesman selects customers/products, submits orders in a mobile-friendly flow under one minute, tracks order status and customer-wise sales. Admin sees incoming orders, verifies/approves/rejects, changes approved quantities/prices, generates GST invoices, records dispatch and payments. Supervisor records purchases/raw materials, manages BOM, records runs, actual output and wastage, and views variance. Owner sees stock, sales, production, cost per packet and receivables. Auditor has read-only monthly reports and exports.
- [ ] **Sales and GST billing:** Customer and product masters; salesman order entry; verification, approval/rejection, order status and customer sales. Invoice contains FY-based invoice number, date, customer, GSTIN, HSN, description, quantity, rate, taxable amount, TN CGST/SGST or interstate IGST, final amount, and PDF download. Posted invoices cannot be deleted; cancellation/credit-note handling is auditable.
- [ ] **BOM and production:** Each product defines raw-material and packaging requirements, expected batch output and standard consumption. Each run records product, batch size, issued materials, expected and actual output, damaged material, wastage and production date. Automatically reduce raw and packaging stock, increase finished stock, calculate planned versus actual consumption and yield variance, and alert above 3%.
- [ ] **Inventory ledger:** Keep separate raw-material, packaging and finished-goods stock. Record opening, purchases, production issues, output, dispatch, returns and adjustments in a stock ledger, each linked to a source document. Do not directly overwrite balances; stock movements are auditable and transactional. Roll back all stock changes if production/dispatch fails.
- [ ] **Costing:** Calculate raw-material, packaging, labour, gas/electricity, total production cost, cost per packet, selling price and margin. Value raw materials at weighted-average rate. Use integer-paisa arithmetic for financial calculations and document the method.
- [ ] **Payments:** Record invoice-linked payments; calculate customer outstanding, partially paid and unpaid invoice status; generate receivables ageing.
- [ ] **Monthly reports and exports:** Provide sales register, customer-wise sales, product-wise sales, GST summary, purchase register, raw-material ledger, packaging ledger, finished-stock report, production report, planned/actual consumption, yield/wastage, cost per packet, collections and outstanding receivables; provide CSV, Excel and PDF exports.
- [ ] **Benchmark seed and workflow:** Exact master data: Murukku 200 g, 200 expected packets/batch, flour 40 kg at new-purchase rate ₹45, oil 10 L at ₹140, spice 1.2 kg at ₹200, pouches 200 at ₹2.50, cartons 10 at ₹15; opening flour 100 kg, oil 30 L, spice 5 kg, pouches 500, cartons 30, finished Murukku 50; selling price ₹50 before 5% GST; Tamil Nadu factory. Execute purchase 60 kg flour at ₹45/kg; one run issuing 40 kg flour, 10.5 L oil, 1.2 kg spice, actual 188 good packets, 6 damaged pouches, 9 cartons; Retailer A TN order 150, approval, invoice, dispatch and ₹4,000 payment; Distributor B Karnataka order 120 and insufficient-stock warning; labour ₹1,200 and gas/power ₹600; generate monthly reports. Preserve expected results: flour 120 kg, oil 19.5 L, spice 3.8 kg, pouches 306, cartons 21, finished stock 238 after production and 88 after dispatch, yield variance -6%, oil consumption variance +5%, Retailer A taxable ₹7,500, CGST ₹187.50, SGST ₹187.50, invoice ₹7,875, outstanding ₹3,875, Distributor B shortage 32. Weighted average flour is (100×₹42+60×₹45)/160=₹43.125/kg. Explain any difference from expected values.
- [ ] **Relational schema, audit and security:** Tables for users, roles, customers, suppliers, products, raw/packaging materials, BOM/items, purchases/items, production runs, stock movements, orders/items, invoices/items, payments, expenses and audit logs, with keys, constraints and indexes. Environment-based credentials and database URL, error handling, audit trail, SQLite initial database with PostgreSQL migration path.
- [ ] **Tests and documentation:** Automated tests for stock arithmetic, GST, production variance and benchmark transaction behavior. Provide complete frontend/backend source, schema, seed data, demo accounts, invoice PDF, reports/exports, setup README, architecture/ER diagram, design note documenting BOM/stock/costing, deployment instructions and an 8-minute demo script. Swagger API docs available.
- [ ] **Responsive industrial dashboard:** Professional modern manufacturing ERP in white/navy with readable typography, desktop/mobile layouts, sidebar navigation, role dashboards, cards, searchable/filterable tables, sales and production charts, stock indicators, production variance alerts and simple fast mobile order entry. All controls connect to real behavior; no fake buttons.
